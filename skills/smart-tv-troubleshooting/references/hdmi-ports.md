---
topic: HDMI port clarification checklist
---

When a user reports HDMI issues on a smart TV app (e.g., cleanyourtv):

- Confirm which ports: HDMI 1, HDMI 2, HDMI 3, ARC, or all.
- Confirm the nature: app-level input mapping, content-level troubleshooting guide, remote switching, or physical/connection issue.
- Confirm whether a repo/workspace is available or if the task is content-only (e.g., "post listed as unavailable").

Never assume the failure is code-level; the user may mean a missing troubleshooting post or broken documentation entry.
