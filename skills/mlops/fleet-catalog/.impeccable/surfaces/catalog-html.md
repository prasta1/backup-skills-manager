# Direction contract — catalog.html

THESIS: The fleet as a monochrome reference — near-black on near-white, one
restrained accent used sparingly, the five questions as clean sections, models
as a precise data table. Refuses the generic dark dashboard and the
claim-and-proof marketing grammar the source world was dealt in.

OWN-WORLD: A fine neutral scale from near-white to near-black (paper ground,
ink text, one or two mid-greys for structure), one restrained accent (oxide
red) used a handful of times — the status/breakage signal and the active
section. Hairline borders, tight geometric sans at two weights (sentence-case
headings, clean body), monospace for model IDs and figures. Generous vertical
rhythm; the page reads as a printed reference, not a screen dashboard.

STORY: Patrick consults the catalog and reads the fleet at a glance — what he
has, which are vision/embedding (or censored/uncensored), the disk cost, what
is broken, and what is left to tune. The five questions are the spine.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A clean masthead ("FLEET CATALOG" in ink, one accent mark), a
quiet summary strip, then the five questions as clearly separated sections —
the text-LLM table, the image-models table, and the fleet-wide tuning view —
each a precise ruled table with mono IDs and measured figures.

FORM: Monochrome product surface (challenger digital-design-canon-monochrome),
adapted from marketing claim-and-proof to a reference-catalog structure.
Seed key 26f17ebb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
