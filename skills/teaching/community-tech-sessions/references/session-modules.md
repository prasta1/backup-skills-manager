# Community Education — Session Modules

Reference templates for one-hour community education sessions.
Each entry is a condensed outline for a self-contained module.

## M1: What is AI, Actually
- Opening hook: show of hands for everyday AI
- Definitions: AI, ML, LLM, agent, model, parameters, prompt, hallucination, local vs cloud
- Core: pattern matching analogy, ChatGPT moment, what it cannot do
- Fear reducer: no agency, no desires, no plan
- Demo: mundane real task + convincing fabrication side by side
- Handout: what we covered, one thing to try, blank questions, coming next

## M2: Agents and Claws Demystified
- Recap from M1
- Chatbot vs agent
- Claw metaphor for tools
- Fragility and limits of agents
- Demo: agent with tool claw, with visible failure
- Fear reducer: agents break constantly, not autonomous

## M3: Running Your Own AI Locally
- Recap from M1-M2
- Why local matters: privacy, cost, control, offline
- Minimum setup: modern Mac/PC, LM Studio, model
- Plain English: 30B parameters
- Demo: LM Studio local vs cloud side by side
- Fear reducer: point-and-click options exist

## M4: Prompts and Reality Checks
- Recap from M1-M3
- Prompting as communication
- Three prompt patterns: drafting brief, transformation, perspective check
- Three reality checks: verify numbers, watch tone without citations, check consistency
- Activity: write one real prompt
- When not to use AI

## M5: AI in Your Town/Workflow
- Recap from M1-M4
- Framework: good tasks vs bad tasks
- Use cases: small business, local government/schools, personal/community
- Group activity: three-bucket list (try next week / try someday / keep human)
- Live prompts based on group-sourced tasks

## M6: Policies, Privacy, and Owning Your Stack
- Recap from M1-M5
- Where cloud data goes
- Open source as practical choice
- Five-question tool evaluation checklist
- Vermont angle
- Extended open Q&A on fears and policy

## Suggested 4-session lightweight series
1. M1 + short M2 overview
2. M4 (practical skill everyone leaves with)
3. M3 or M5 depending on interest
4. M6 with open Q&A on fears and policy
