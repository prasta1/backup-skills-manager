# Feeler Post Template for Community Education Series

Use this before drafting full curriculum. The goal is to confirm real interest before investing session-writing time.

## Template structure
- One sentence on what the series is
- Topic lines or module names
- Format/duration statement
- A line on motivation ("community demystification, not sales")
- A single feedback prompt: "Which of these would you show up for?"

## Example (Front Porch Forum / email newsletter)
I'm putting together a short community series on artificial intelligence: what it actually is, how it works, how to use it safely, and how to run AI tools locally without sending your data to the cloud.

Possible sessions:
- What is AI, actually
- Agents and tools demystified
- Running your own AI locally
- Prompts and reality checks
- AI in your town or workflow
- Privacy, policy, and owning your stack

One hour per session, whatever people want to show up for. I'm doing this because I think our community deserves better than hype or fear.

Which of these would you show up for?

## Notes
- Keep it to six topics max
- One feedback prompt beats five because fewer choices = more responses
- Include the direct "not sales" framing to reduce suspicion early
