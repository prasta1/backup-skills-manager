# Art Directions

Pick ONE as the starting recipe; adapt everything to the specific business.
Two same-category businesses in the same town must not get near-identical
sites — vary layout and palette within the direction.

## 1. Food & casual eats (Burger Barn, Erica's Diner, Taste of Texas)
- Mood: warm, appetite-first, a little playful. The food is the hero.
- Type: chunky display (Fraunces, Bricolage Grotesque, or Alfarn-style slab)
  + clean sans body (Inter, Work Sans).
- Color: appetite palette pulled from their food photos — deep reds, charred
  oranges, mustard, cream. Dark backgrounds work for BBQ/burgers.
- Layout: full-bleed food hero with name + one-liner + hours badge →
  menu highlights (3–6 items, prices if known) → story strip → reviews →
  find-us (map + hours + click-to-call).
- Signature moves: hand-drawn accents, sticker/badge shapes, "cash only —
  worth it" style honesty.
- Avoid: white-tablecloth elegance, stock burgers, corporate blue.

## 2. General & country stores (Hanley's, Fletcher, Jeffersonville Country)
- Mood: nostalgic-but-fresh Vermont. The store as community fixture.
- Type: sturdy serif headlines (Libre Caslon, Lora, Playfair) + humanist sans
  body (Source Sans 3, PT Sans).
- Color: textured paper/cream base, forest green, barn red, warm brown.
- Layout: storefront or interior hero → "since <year>" story first →
  what-you'll-find grid (departments/goods) → seasonal callout (creemees,
  maple, hunting tags) → reviews → visit (map/hours).
- Signature moves: subtle paper texture, hand-painted-sign energy in
  headings, local landmarks in copy.
- Avoid: minimalist tech aesthetic, cold grays.

## 3. Salons & personal care (Salon Specifics, Eclipse, Fern Hill Massage)
- Mood: airy, editorial, calm confidence.
- Type: elegant high-contrast serif or fashion sans (Cormorant, Marcellus,
  Jost) + light body.
- Color: soft neutrals — blush, sage, sand, ivory — one deep accent.
- Layout: generous-whitespace hero (their work or space) → services + prices
  menu (typographic, not cards) → about-the-owner (first person) → reviews →
  book-now CTA repeated, click-to-call.
- Signature moves: thin rules, uppercase letterspaced labels, oversized
  section numerals.
- Avoid: clutter, more than 2 typefaces, garish gradients.

## 4. Trades & auto (A.J. Automotive, Woody's, Ace Glass, J & L Hardware)
- Mood: solid, trustworthy, no-nonsense. Answers "can I trust them?" fast.
- Type: bold condensed headings (Archivo, Barlow Condensed, Oswald) +
  plain body (Inter, Roboto).
- Color: one strong workwear color (navy, safety orange, forest) + charcoal
  + white. High contrast.
- Layout: hero = name, what they do in plain words, years-in-business badge,
  BIG click-to-call button → services as plain-spoken list → why-us proof
  (local, honest, since <year>) → reviews as trust anchor → map/hours.
- Signature moves: credential badges, phone number visible in nav at all
  times, "no phone tree — you get <owner>" honesty.
- Avoid: decorative fonts, thin gray text, anything precious.

## 5. Gifts & specialty retail (Craft Nook, Live-N-Comfort, TopdecK)
- Mood: personality-heavy; the shop's own aesthetic drives everything.
- Type: characterful display matched to their vibe (quirky for a card shop,
  handmade for crafts) + simple body.
- Color: sample directly from their product photos; let products carry it.
- Layout: product-collage or single-hero → what-we-carry categories →
  Instagram-grid gallery (their real posts) → owner story → visit.
- Signature moves: IG-native feel, product close-ups, event/new-arrivals
  callout if they post those.
- Avoid: imposing a palette that fights their merchandise.
