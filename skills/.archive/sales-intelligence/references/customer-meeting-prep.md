# Customer Meeting Prep Toolkit

## Quick Setup
- Pull data from: HubSpot (deals/companies/contacts), Amplitude (usage), Edge Impulse (project status)
- Output: structured briefing with key metrics and conversation starters

## Pre-Meeting Research Template

### Company Snapshot
- Current deal stage and forecast
- Key contacts and their roles
- Recent engagement history (emails/meetings)
- Company size/tier changes

### Usage Analysis
- Product adoption trends (last 30/60/90 days)
- Feature usage depth
- Anomaly or decline indicators
- Power user identification

### Project Status (Edge Impulse)
- Active deployments and last update
- Model performance trends
- Expansion opportunities (data volume, projects)
- Red flags (failed deployments, no activity)

## Conversation Starters Framework

### Expansion Opportunities
- Use: "I noticed your team's [FEATURE] usage has grown 40% MoM..."
- Use: "Your data volume is approaching the [TIER] threshold..."
- Use: "We have 3 other customers in [INDUSTRY] seeing success with..."

### Risk Mitigation
- Use: "I see [FEATURE] usage has declined, is there anything blocking..."
- Use: "Let's discuss your deployment challenges..."
- Use: "Are you getting value from [RECENT PURCHASE]..."

### Agenda Suggestions
- Review: Q1 usage trends and adoption goals
- Discuss: scaling deployment and compute needs
- Plan: next phase project roadmap
- Align: on renewal timeline and expansion opportunities
