**Caveat before you use a name:** roles are as recorded *at the time*. No current
employer, title or contactability has been verified for anyone on either list. Reid
against LinkedIn (or Patrick) before drafting, and never assert a current title in a
first line — the whole point of the note is that the recipient remembers him warmly,
and a stale title spends that goodwill.

## How to use it

1. Pick 5–8 names, not all of them. A blast to 18 accounts reads as a mail merge.
2. For each, identify the actual ask: an intro to a specific company in the AI/ML-infra
   lane, or just "keep me in mind." Variant choice follows from that.
3. Log who was contacted and when. This list has no state of its own — a second pass
   over the same name is the failure mode.
