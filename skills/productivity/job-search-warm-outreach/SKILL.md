# Job Search Warm Outreach

Use when Patrick needs a concise, Patrick-voiced note to a former customer or colleague asking for a warm intro — aimed at **Account Executive roles in AI/ML infrastructure**, not the CS lane.

## Trigger
- Patrick mentions reaching out to a former customer/contact after being let go
- Patrick asks for a draft note to keep someone in mind for an AE / AI-infrastructure role
- Any "former customer outreach" or "warm intro request" ask

## Output
One short draft in Patrick's voice, plus a lane/context paragraph. No fluff, no apology theater, no overselling.

## Patrick's Parameters (standing)
- **Target lane: Account Executive — AI/ML infrastructure and open-source-AI-adjacent companies, Seed–C.** This replaced the VP/Director/Head of CS anchor on 2026-09-18. Do not pitch the CS lane.
- Floor: $200K+ base
- Location: remote or Vermont preferred
- Style: direct, warm but not performative, assumes the recipient remembers him positively

**Positioning note — lead with the selling outcome, not the title.** His résumé leads with "VP of Customer Success," which for an early-stage AE role can read as over-senior or non-quota. What actually qualifies him: 10+ years carrying enterprise relationships and technical evaluations for AI products (Edge Impulse, DataRobot), and a final Qualcomm role that *was* net-new logo AE work. The line that does the work — he can run a technical evaluation **and** still own the account after signature, which is what a Seed–C AI-infra team needs from an early AE. Don't hide the CS background; frame it as why complex, technical deals don't scare him.

**Target list:** `references/former-team-and-accounts.md` — the former team and the 18 accounts covered in the Edge Impulse/Qualcomm era. Read it with its caveat: no current title or contact status is verified for anyone on it.

## Three Variants

**Variant A: LinkedIn-style / Short**
> [Name] — hope [company/product] is going well. Quick heads-up: I was let go from Qualcomm on [date], after the Edge Impulse acquisition, and I'm back on the market. I'm targeting AE roles at AI-infrastructure companies, Seed to C, remote or Vermont. If you're talking to any of them, I'd appreciate the intro. Background: 10+ years selling and running enterprise CS/BD for technical AI products — Edge Impulse, DataRobot.

Use when: the relationship was professional but not deeply personal, LinkedIn is the likely channel, recipient is busy.

**Variant B: Warmer**
> [Name], quick heads-up: I was let go from Qualcomm on [date] and I'm looking for my next AE role — specifically AI infrastructure, Seed to C, remote or Vermont. With [company]'s [product/market position], you're probably talking to a lot of these teams. If any of them need someone who can run a technical evaluation and still own the account after signature, keep me in mind. Happy to send a resume or just talk through where I'd add leverage.

Use when: rapport was good, recipient is likely still in founder/operator circles, feels like a natural conversation.

**Variant C: Prior relationship front-and-center**
> [Name] — you may remember me from [prior company], where we worked on [account or project]. I was let go on [date] and I'm back in the market for AE roles at AI-infrastructure companies, Seed to C, remote or VT. The work we did together is the kind of account I want to be selling into. If any of the startups you work with need that, I'd value a warm intro.

Use when: the prior relationship is the main hook and should be front-and-center.

## Placeholders
- [Name] — first name, unless Patrick uses a nickname
- [company] — the contact's current company
- [product/market position] — one line on what they're building, if relevant
- [prior company] — Edge Impulse or Qualcomm, whichever they'd know him by
- [account or project] — the shared account or work, for Variant C only
- [date] — month + day is enough

## Delivery Guidance
- Channel: LinkedIn message or email, whichever is Patrick's normal touchpoint with the person
- Length: one paragraph, under 150 words if possible
- CTA: either "I'd appreciate the intro" (low-friction) or "keep me in mind" (even lower friction)
- Don't attach a resume in the first message; offer to share on reply
- If Patrick is unsure which variant, default to Variant B
- **One send per person.** A second ask to the same name without new information reads as pressure; log who has been contacted.

## After Drafting
Patrick usually sends these himself. Do not send on his behalf unless explicitly asked.
